Behind the publication

The person behind the perspective.

I am Dakshaya Chaudhary, a independent journalist and the person behind The Insight Journal. I created this publication to explore the stories, questions, and ideas that deserve a closer look.

About me

I am an independent journalist interested in understanding the world beyond headlines. For me, journalism is about asking thoughtful questions, examining evidence, and making complicated subjects easier for readers to understand.

I am developing my approach to reporting, research, and writing through The Insight Journal. I want my work to be clear and accessible while still taking facts, context, and different perspectives seriously.

I believe that being curious is a useful starting point, but curiosity must be accompanied by careful research and a willingness to correct mistakes.

Why I founded The Insight Journal

The Insight Journal was created as a space to examine current issues, everyday experiences, and ideas that affect society. I wanted a publication where I could investigate questions, organise information, and communicate what I learn in my own words.

My aim is not simply to repeat what is already being said. It is to look for context, consider the evidence, and help readers understand why a subject matters.

As the publication develops, I hope to keep improving the quality of its reporting and make each article more useful, thoughtful, and transparent.

What I write about

My interests span several areas. I am particularly interested in how public decisions, social changes, and new technologies affect people's lives.

News and public affairs: Understanding developments, the context behind them, and the questions readers should consider.

Society: Exploring everyday issues, changing attitudes, and the experiences that shape communities.

Education: Examining learning, educational policies, and the experiences of students and teachers.

Technology: Looking at how tools such as artificial intelligence influence learning, work, communication, and society.

What guides my journalism

Independent journalism requires more than having an opinion. It requires a commitment to examining whether that opinion is supported by evidence. These are the principles I aim to follow in my work.

Accuracy over speed. Important claims should be checked before publication, and uncertainty should be acknowledged rather than hidden.

Evidence over assumptions. Facts, interpretations, and personal opinions should be distinguishable. Statistics and quotations should be traceable to their sources wherever possible.

Fairness and context. People and issues should be represented fairly. Relevant context should not be omitted simply because it complicates a story.

Transparency. Sources, limitations, and corrections matter. If an article contains a significant error, it should be corrected openly.

Responsibility. Reporting should respect privacy, treat people with dignity, and take care when dealing with sensitive subjects or unverified allegations.
