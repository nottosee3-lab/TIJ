ABOUT THE PUBLICATION

A small publication. A serious purpose.

The Insight Journal (TIJ) is an independent journalism project focused on research, reporting, current affairs and thoughtful analysis.

Our aim is to make complicated topics easier to understand while being transparent about evidence, uncertainty and corrections.

OUR COMMITMENT

Editorial standards

Accuracy

Verify important claims before publication.

Source credit

Link to original documents and credit reporting used.

Fairness

Separate verified facts, interview quotes and personal opinion.

Corrections

Correct errors transparently when they are discovered.
