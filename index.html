Who we are

Curiosity with a purpose.

The Insight Journal is an independent publication exploring public affairs, technology, society and the questions behind the news. Our aim is to make issues understandable, examine claims carefully and give readers a clearer view of the facts.

Trust is earned

How we approach a story.

Our principles should be visible in the reporting, not just printed at the bottom of the page.

01 / ACCURACY

Check the claim.

Prefer reliable primary sources, verify important facts and distinguish established evidence from interpretation.

02 / TRANSPARENCY

Show the sources.

Credit sources and link readers to relevant documents wherever practical. Make uncertainty clear.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Correct mistakes.

If a material error is identified, correct it clearly and explain the change when appropriate.
